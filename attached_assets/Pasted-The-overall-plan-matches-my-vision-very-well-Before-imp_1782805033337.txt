The overall plan matches my vision very well. Before implementation, I want to make a few refinements so we build the product exactly as intended.

1. Theme Selection Flow (Important)

Please change the flow.

Instead of:

Upload → Processing → Theme Selection → Dashboard

I want:

Upload DPR → Choose Theme → Finny Processing Experience → Dashboard

The user should choose the report theme before AI starts generating the dashboard. That selected theme must then be applied throughout the processing experience, the dashboard shell, and the exported PDF.

2. Finny Processing Experience

Our AI extraction is already very fast (around 5–6 seconds), but I want the product to feel premium rather than instantaneous.

Instead of jumping directly to the dashboard, create a beautiful Finny experience lasting around 25–30 seconds.

The stages should feel cinematic rather than pretending to show exact backend progress.

Example journey:

👋 Hi! I'm Finny. Let me read your DPR.
🤔 Understanding your business...
💻 Analyzing financial statements...
📊 Finding key financial insights...
📄 Designing your beautiful dashboard...
🎉 Report Ready!

The animation should complete naturally and then open the dashboard.

This is a product experience, not a fake loading bar.
